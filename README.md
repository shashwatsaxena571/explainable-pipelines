## What is this?

I'm a **Data Engineer at IBM** (working with a large banking client) and a **PhD scholar in Trustworthy & Explainable AI** at VIT.

My core thesis, from both sides of that fence:

> **You can't explain a model if you can't trace your data. Explainability starts in the pipeline, not the model.**

Every week, the newsletter covers one regulation or research shift, one architecture pattern, and one thing to check in your own pipelines. This repo holds the code, diagrams, and sources behind each edition.
